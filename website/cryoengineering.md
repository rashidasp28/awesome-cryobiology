# Cryoengineering

Cryoengineering focuses on the thermal and mechanical systems required for controlled low-temperature biology.

---

# Core Areas

## Cryostages

Topics include:
- directional freezing
- thermal gradients
- optical compatibility
- anti-frost systems

---

## Thermal Systems

Topics include:
- cooling profiles
- warming profiles
- thermal mapping
- thermal validation

---

## LN2 Systems

Topics include:
- liquid nitrogen delivery
- insulation
- safety systems
- flow control

---

## Sensor Integration

Examples:
- RTDs
- thermocouples
- thermal imaging
- environmental monitoring

---

## Microscope Compatibility

Challenges include:
- objective thermal exchange
- vibration reduction
- condensation control
- immersion compatibility
