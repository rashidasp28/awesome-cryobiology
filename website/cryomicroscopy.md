# Cryomicroscopy

Cryomicroscopy combines:

- cryobiology,
- thermal engineering,
- microscopy,
- fluorescence imaging,
- and computational image analysis.

---

# Core Imaging Modalities

## Brightfield Cryomicroscopy

Applications:
- ice crystal visualization
- directional freezing
- freezing front tracking

---

## Fluorescence Cryomicroscopy

Applications:
- viability imaging
- ROS imaging
- membrane integrity analysis

---

## Confocal Cryomicroscopy

Applications:
- optical sectioning
- high-resolution imaging
- 3D cryoinjury analysis

---

## Multiphoton Cryomicroscopy

Applications:
- deep tissue imaging
- reduced phototoxicity
- real-time cryoinjury analysis
- redox imaging
