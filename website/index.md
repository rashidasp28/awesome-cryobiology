# Awesome Cryobiology

## The Open Knowledge Hub for Cryobiology

A curated open platform for:

- cryobiology,
- cryopreservation,
- cryomicroscopy,
- cryoengineering,
- and AI-assisted low-temperature biology.

---

# Why This Platform Exists

Cryobiology knowledge is often fragmented across:

- papers,
- imaging workflows,
- engineering systems,
- protocols,
- and computational pipelines.

Awesome Cryobiology aims to bridge these areas into a unified educational and scientific ecosystem.
