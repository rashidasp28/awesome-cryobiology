## Cryoinjury Pathways

![Cryoinjury Pathways](../figures/cryoinjury-pathways.svg)

This diagram summarizes major pathways of cryoinjury, including extracellular ice formation, osmotic stress, intracellular ice formation, membrane damage, and reduced viability.

## Slow Freezing vs Vitrification

![Slow Freezing vs Vitrification](../figures/slow-freezing-vs-vitrification.svg)
