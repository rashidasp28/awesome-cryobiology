# Open Cryobiology Protocols

This collection provides structured experimental workflows and reproducible protocols for cryobiology, cryomicroscopy, cryoengineering, and fluorescence imaging.

The goal is to support:

- reproducibility,
- education,
- protocol standardization,
- and collaborative open science.

---
