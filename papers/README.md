# Papers

A curated entry point for landmark and review literature in cryobiology, cryopreservation, cryomicroscopy, and cryoengineering.

## How to Use This Page

Start with review papers and classic theory, then move into application-specific literature.

## Core Reading Themes

### Cryobiology Fundamentals

- Freezing injury mechanisms
- Water transport and osmotic stress
- Intracellular ice formation
- Solution effects injury
- Cooling and warming rate optimization

Recommended classic authors and themes:

- Peter Mazur - cell freezing, intracellular ice, and cooling-rate theory
- Harold T. Meryman - cryobiology principles and osmotic injury
- James Lovelock - early work on glycerol and cell preservation
