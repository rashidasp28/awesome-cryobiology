# Cryobiology Troubleshooting Guide

A practical troubleshooting guide for cryopreservation, cryomicroscopy, fluorescence imaging, thermal systems, and computational analysis.

---

# Low Post-Thaw Viability

## Possible Causes

- Excessive cooling rate
- Excessive warming delay
- Intracellular ice formation
- CPA toxicity
- Osmotic stress
- Inadequate equilibration

## Potential Solutions

- Optimize cooling profile
- Increase warming rate
- Adjust CPA exposure time
- Reduce osmotic shock
- Validate thermal profiles

---

# Excessive Ice Formation

## Possible Causes

- Insufficient CPA concentration
- Slow cooling in vitrification workflows
- Thermal instability
- Poor cryostage insulation

## Potential Solutions

- Increase vitrification efficiency
- Improve thermal control
- Validate temperature gradients
- Improve sample geometry consistency

---

# Devitrification During Warming

## Possible Causes

- Slow warming rate
- Thermal nonuniformity
- Inadequate warming systems

## Potential Solutions

- Increase warming rate
- Reduce sample volume
- Improve thermal coupling
- Explore nanowarming strategies

---

# Frost and Condensation on Cryostage

## Possible Causes

- Humidity exposure
- Inadequate dry-gas purge
- Poor vacuum isolation

## Potential Solutions

- Improve dry-gas systems
- Use vacuum shrouds
- Reduce environmental humidity
- Minimize stage exposure time

---

# Weak Fluorescence Signal

## Possible Causes

- Incorrect excitation wavelength
- Low fluorophore concentration
- Photobleaching
- Inappropriate filters
- Thermal effects on fluorophores

## Potential Solutions

- Validate excitation and emission compatibility
- Optimize laser power
- Adjust detector gain
- Reduce photobleaching exposure
- Verify fluorophore compatibility under cryogenic conditions

---

# High Fluorescence Background

## Possible Causes

- Media autofluorescence
- Egg-yolk interference
- Spectral overlap
- Frost artifacts

## Potential Solutions

- Optimize imaging medium
- Adjust filters
- Reduce background fluorescence
- Validate fluorophore separation

---

# Sample Drift During Imaging

## Possible Causes

- Thermal expansion
- Poor mounting
- Mechanical instability
- Ice movement

## Potential Solutions

- Improve stage stability
- Use immobilization strategies
- Optimize thermal equilibration
- Reduce vibration sources

---

# Segmentation Failure in AI Workflows

## Possible Causes

- Poor annotations

- Inconsistent imaging conditions

## Potential Solutions

- Improve annotation quality
- Increase dataset diversity
- Standardize acquisition settings
- Use preprocessing pipelines
