# Curation Criteria

This document defines the quality standards used for selecting resources in Awesome Cryobiology.

## Repository Philosophy

The goal is not to collect the largest number of links.

The goal is to curate:
- scientifically valuable,
- educationally useful,
- reproducible,
- and practically relevant resources.

Quality is prioritized over quantity.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

## Scientific Credibility

Resources should:
- come from credible journals, institutions, repositories, or communities,
- be technically accurate,
- and provide meaningful scientific or engineering value.

---

## Educational Value

Resources that clearly explain:
- concepts,
- workflows,
- engineering systems,
- or analysis methods
are strongly encouraged.

---

## Reproducibility

Preference is given to resources that include:
- metadata,
- protocols,
- datasets,
- code,
- or methodological transparency.

---

## Accessibility

Preference is given to:
- open-access papers,
- open-source tools,
- reusable educational materials,
- and publicly accessible datasets.

---

## Technical Utility

Useful resources include:
- protocols,
- software,
- workflows,
- thermal validation methods,
- segmentation pipelines,
- and engineering documentation.

---

## Licensing and Reuse

Resources involving:
- figures,
- datasets,
- code,
- or educational material
should have clearly reusable licensing where possible.

---

# Recommended Resource Types

Strongly encouraged:

- landmark papers
- review papers
- protocols
- open datasets
- software tools
- AI workflows
- cryomicroscopy guides
- cryostage engineering resources
- educational diagrams
- reproducible pipelines

---

# Discouraged Content

The following are discouraged:

- broken links
- unverified claims
- duplicate resources
- poorly documented repositories
- inaccessible commercial resources without educational value
- low-quality AI-generated scientific content without validation

---

# Long-Term Goal

Awesome Cryobiology aims to become:
- a trusted scientific resource,
- a reproducibility-focused platform,
- and a collaborative ecosystem for cryobiology and cryomicroscopy.
