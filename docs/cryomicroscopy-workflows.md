# Cryomicroscopy Workflows

A curated guide to cryomicroscopy workflows used in cryobiology, cryopreservation, thermal imaging, and real-time cryoinjury analysis.

---

# Brightfield Cryomicroscopy

## Typical Applications

- Ice crystal visualization
- Freezing front tracking
- Directional freezing studies
- Ice morphology analysis

## Typical Workflow

1. Sample preparation
2. CPA loading
3. Mounting on cryostage
4. Controlled cooling
5. Time-lapse imaging
6. Warming and recovery imaging
7. Image analysis

---

# Fluorescence Cryomicroscopy

## Typical Applications

- Membrane integrity
- ROS imaging
- Mitochondrial activity
- Cryoinjury localization

## Typical Fluorophores

- Propidium iodide
- Calcein AM
- SYBR-14
- CellMask dyes
- ROS probes

## Typical Workflow

1. Fluorophore staining
2. CPA equilibration
3. Cryostage mounting
4. Thermal control
5. Fluorescence acquisition
6. Viability analysis

---

# Confocal Cryomicroscopy

## Typical Applications

- Optical sectioning
- High-resolution fluorescence imaging
- 3D cryoinjury analysis

## Considerations

- Thermal drift
- Frost suppression
- Objective compatibility
- Refractive-index mismatch

---

# Multiphoton Cryomicroscopy

## Typical Applications

- Deep imaging
- Reduced phototoxicity
- Live-cell cryoinjury imaging
- Redox biology
- Thick sample imaging

## Common Laser Systems

- 780 nm systems
- 1064 nm systems

## Important Considerations

- Laser power calibration
- Thermal exchange with objectives
- Water immersion challenges
- Signal-to-noise optimization
- Frost control

---

# Thermal Gradient Cryomicroscopy

## Typical Applications

- Directional freezing
- Moving freezing fronts
- Ice-interface dynamics
- Thermal mapping

## Typical Components

- Cold block
- Warm block
- RTDs or thermocouples
- Motion systems
- Cryostage channel
