A curated collection of cryobiology, cryopreservation, cryomicroscopy, cryoengineering, and low-temperature biology resources including software, datasets, protocols, papers, conferences, and open hardware.

## Vision

Cryobiology knowledge is often fragmented across papers, protocols, laboratories, engineering systems, and specialized communities. This repository aims to become an open entry point for researchers, students, engineers, microscopy specialists, AI developers, and biobanking professionals working across low-temperature biology.

**Positioning:** The Open Knowledge Hub for Cryobiology.

This section is for students, early-career researchers, engineers entering cryobiology, and scientists moving into cryopreservation research.

8. **Sperm cryopreservation** - Reviews on extenders, membrane integrity, motility, ROS, and fertility outcomes.
9. **Stem cell cryopreservation** - Resources on HSCs, iPSCs, organoids, CAR-T cells, and biobanking.

## Fundamentals

Resources on cryobiology principles, low-temperature biology, thermodynamics, water physics, and preservation science.

- [Society for Cryobiology](https://www.societyforcryobiology.org/) - International society supporting cryobiology research, conferences, education, and community-building.
- [Cryobiology journal](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/cryobiology) - Major journal covering low-temperature biology, cryopreservation, vitrification, cryoinjury, and related technologies.
- [Low Temperature Biology](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/low-temperature-biology-of-insects/35A616A4C1111CDA91E7330240F10034) - Useful background for biological responses to low temperature, especially in natural systems.
- **Mazur, P.** - Foundational work on freezing injury, intracellular ice formation, and cell survival during cooling.
- **Meryman, H. T.** - Classic contributions to cryobiology, osmotic stress, and preservation principles.

## Cryoprotective Agents

Resources on CPAs, their mechanisms, toxicity, osmotic effects, and use in cryopreservation protocols.

- **DMSO** - Widely used permeating CPA for mammalian cells, stem cells, immune cells, and cell therapy products.
- **Glycerol** - Major CPA in sperm cryopreservation and red blood cell preservation.
- **Ethylene glycol** - Commonly used in embryo and oocyte vitrification protocols.
- **Propylene glycol** - Permeating CPA used in some embryo and oocyte cryopreservation systems.
- **Trehalose** - Non-permeating sugar investigated for membrane stabilization and dehydration protection.
- **Sucrose** - Non-permeating osmotic agent often used in vitrification and warming solutions.
- **Antifreeze proteins and glycoproteins** - Ice-binding biomolecules that influence ice growth and recrystallization.
- **Ice recrystallization inhibitors** - Synthetic and biological molecules designed to limit ice crystal growth during storage and warming.

## Ice Recrystallization

Resources on ice recrystallization mechanisms, microscopy assays, and ice recrystallization inhibitors.

- **Splat cooling assay** - Common assay for evaluating ice recrystallization inhibition activity.
- **Cryomicroscopy-based recrystallization analysis** - Direct imaging approach for monitoring crystal growth over time.
- **ImageJ/Fiji workflows** - Useful for crystal size measurement, segmentation, and time-series analysis.
- **IRI-active polymers** - Synthetic polymers explored as antifreeze-protein mimics.
- **Antifreeze proteins** - Biological ice-binding proteins used as models for recrystallization inhibition.

## Devitrification

Resources on devitrification during warming, glass stability, thermal modeling, and high-warming-rate systems.

- **Vitrification theory** - Resources on glass formation, critical cooling rates, and critical warming rates.
- **Devitrification during warming** - Studies on ice formation during rewarming of vitrified samples.
- **Nanowarming** - Rapid and volumetric warming strategy using nanoparticles and electromagnetic fields.
- **High-warming-rate systems** - Devices and protocols designed to reduce devitrification risk.
- **Thermal modeling** - Heat-transfer models for predicting cooling, warming, and thermal gradients.

## Cryoinjury

Resources on biological damage during cooling, freezing, storage, and warming.

- **Intracellular ice formation** - Major mechanism of lethal cryoinjury in many cell types.
- **Solution effects injury** - Damage from solute concentration, dehydration, and osmotic stress during freezing.
- **Membrane phase transitions** - Temperature-induced membrane changes affecting permeability and integrity.
- **Oxidative stress** - ROS-associated injury during cooling, freezing, storage, and rewarming.
- **Mitochondrial dysfunction** - Mitochondrial damage linked to reduced post-thaw viability and function.
- **Rewarming injury** - Damage that occurs during thawing or warming, including devitrification and osmotic shock.

## Cryomicroscopy and Imaging

Resources on imaging biological systems at low temperature.

- [Fiji/ImageJ](https://imagej.net/software/fiji/) - General microscopy image analysis platform useful for cryomicroscopy workflows.
- [napari](https://napari.org/) - Multidimensional image viewer useful for time-lapse, volumetric, and annotated microscopy data.
- [Micro-Manager](https://micro-manager.org/) - Open-source microscopy control software for automated acquisition.
- [Bio-Formats](https://www.openmicroscopy.org/bio-formats/) - Tool for reading proprietary microscopy file formats.
- [OME-Zarr](https://ngff.openmicroscopy.org/latest/) - Cloud-friendly format for large bioimaging datasets.
- **Brightfield cryomicroscopy** - Useful for observing ice formation, morphology, freezing fronts, and recrystallization.
- **Fluorescence cryomicroscopy** - Useful for viability, membrane integrity, ROS, and organelle-specific assays.
- **Confocal microscopy** - Useful for optical sectioning and fluorescence localization.
- **Multiphoton microscopy** - Useful for deeper imaging, reduced out-of-focus excitation, and nonlinear fluorescence strategies.
- **Cryo-EM** - Structural biology technique for vitrified biological specimens.

## Sperm Cryopreservation

Resources on sperm preservation across species, including protocols, extenders, viability assays, and imaging methods.

- **Human sperm cryopreservation** - Fertility preservation, assisted reproduction, motility, and post-thaw viability.
- **Ram sperm cryopreservation** - Extenders, egg-yolk-based media, glycerol, membrane integrity, and fertility outcomes.
- **Bull sperm cryopreservation** - Commercial artificial insemination workflows, CASA, motility, and extender optimization.
- **Goat sperm cryopreservation** - Species-specific extender and membrane sensitivity considerations.
- **Fish sperm cryopreservation** - Aquaculture, conservation, and species-specific protocols.
- **Poultry sperm cryopreservation** - Avian reproductive preservation and semen extender challenges.
- **CASA systems** - Computer-assisted sperm analysis for motility and kinetic parameters.
- **Propidium iodide** - Common membrane-impermeant dye for identifying membrane-compromised sperm cells.
- **SYBR-14/PI assay** - Common live/dead sperm viability staining combination.
- **JC-1 and mitochondrial probes** - Used for mitochondrial membrane potential assessment.

## Stem Cells and Cell Therapy

Resources on cryopreservation for cell therapy, regenerative medicine, and biobanking.

- **Hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells** - Clinically important cryopreservation model for transplantation and biobanking.
- **Induced pluripotent stem cells** - Important for regenerative medicine, disease modeling, and cell banks.
- **Organoids** - Three-dimensional culture systems requiring optimized preservation methods.
- **CAR-T cells** - Cell therapy products requiring controlled cryopreservation and post-thaw potency.
- **Primary cells** - Sensitive biological materials requiring cell-type-specific protocols.
- **Biobanking workflows** - Standardized processing, storage, documentation, quality control, and traceability.
- **GMP-compatible cryopreservation** - Regulatory-grade workflows for clinical and translational applications.

## Software and Computational Tools

### Image Analysis

- [Fiji/ImageJ](https://imagej.net/software/fiji/) - Open-source image processing platform widely used in microscopy.
- [napari](https://napari.org/) - Python-based multidimensional image viewer.
- [ilastik](https://www.ilastik.org/) - Interactive machine learning for image classification and segmentation.
- [Cellpose](https://www.cellpose.org/) - Generalist deep learning model for cell and object segmentation.
- [StarDist](https://github.com/stardist/stardist) - Deep learning tool for star-convex object detection.
- [DeepCell](https://deepcell.org/) - Deep learning platform for single-cell image analysis.
- [QuPath](https://qupath.github.io/) - Open-source software for bioimage analysis, especially large microscopy images.
- [scikit-image](https://scikit-image.org/) - Python library for image processing.
- [OpenCV](https://opencv.org/) - Computer vision library useful for segmentation, tracking, and feature extraction.

### Microscopy Data Management

- [Bio-Formats](https://www.openmicroscopy.org/bio-formats/) - Reads many microscopy image formats.
- [OMERO](https://www.openmicroscopy.org/omero/) - Platform for managing, sharing, and analyzing microscopy data.
- [OME-Zarr](https://ngff.openmicroscopy.org/latest/) - Next-generation format for large multidimensional bioimaging datasets.

### Cryo-EM and Structural Biology

- [CryoSPARC](https://cryosparc.com/) - Cryo-EM data processing platform.
- [RELION](https://relion.readthedocs.io/) - Software for cryo-EM structure determination.

## AI and Machine Learning

Applications of AI in cryobiology and cryomicroscopy:

- [Cellpose](https://www.cellpose.org/) - Generalist segmentation model for cells and microscopy objects.
- [StarDist](https://github.com/stardist/stardist) - Object detection and segmentation for star-convex objects.
- [ilastik](https://www.ilastik.org/) - Interactive machine learning for classification and segmentation.
- [DeepCell](https://deepcell.org/) - Deep learning tools for single-cell analysis.
- [YOLO](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics) - Object detection and segmentation framework that can be adapted for microscopy tasks.
- [Segment Anything](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything) - Foundation segmentation model that may support annotation workflows.
- **Cell segmentation** - Detecting and segmenting cells during freezing and warming.
- **Sperm detection and tracking** - Automated counting, motility analysis, and viability classification.
- **Ice crystal segmentation** - Quantifying ice morphology, growth, and recrystallization.
- **Cryoinjury prediction** - Linking imaging, thermal history, and post-thaw outcomes.
- **Thermal process optimization** - Using data-driven models to optimize cooling and warming protocols.

## Protocols

Protocol categories and starting points:

- [Bio-protocol](https://bio-protocol.org/) - Open-access life science protocol platform.
- [protocols.io](https://www.protocols.io/) - Platform for sharing and discovering scientific protocols.
- **CPA loading and unloading** - Osmotic management, equilibration, and stepwise dilution.
- **Controlled-rate freezing** - Cooling profiles, seeding, hold steps, and thermal records.
- **Vitrification** - High CPA exposure, rapid cooling, warming, and dilution.
- **Cryomicroscopy preparation** - Sample chamber design, coverslip preparation, sealing, and imaging conditions.
- **Viability staining** - Live/dead assays, membrane integrity, mitochondrial probes, ROS assays.
- **Ice recrystallization assays** - Splat cooling, annealing, imaging, and crystal size analysis.
- **Sperm cryopreservation** - Extender preparation, equilibration, freezing, thawing, and post-thaw assessment.
- **Stem cell cryopreservation** - Freezing medium, controlled-rate cooling, thawing, recovery, and potency testing.
