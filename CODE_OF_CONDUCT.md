# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating an open, welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for researchers, students, engineers, and contributors from all backgrounds.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Welcome newcomers
- Support open scientific collaboration
- Give proper credit to contributors and sources
- Encourage reproducibility and transparency

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Spam or misleading contributions
- Deliberate misinformation

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove contributions or restrict participation where necessary to maintain a healthy and collaborative environment.
